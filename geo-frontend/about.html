About CountryInfo

Making global demographic and geographic data simple, structured, and accessible for everyone.

Our Mission

CountryInfo was created to have an intuitive webpage for keeping track of stats across every continent. Whether you are looking for population metrics, land area comparisons, or capital details, our goal is to deliver clear facts at a glance without clutter.

Comprehensive Data

Our site breaks down country metrics, from GDP and population density to capital cities and total land area. Interactive continent maps and alphabetized directories help you find exactly what you're searching for in seconds.
